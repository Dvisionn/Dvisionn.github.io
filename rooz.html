Rooz Studio

Identity, icon system and environmental graphics for a women's fitness studio.

Client
Rooz Studio
Year
2024
Scope
Logo, icon system, merchandise, print, wall graphics
Audience
Women

In the space

Wall graphics run through the studio at the points where people stop moving — the free weight area, the wall opposite the bikes. They are the only place the brand gets to be funny.

The identity needed to stay simple and unassuming — humble, but still carrying motion, energy and health. Green gradients and organic shapes do that: curved forms rather than rigid geometry.

The mark

A figure and a leaf sharing a single stroke, drawn so the two never quite separate. The head reads as a seed, the raised arm reads as a stem. It works as a stamp on a bottle at eight millimetres and as a two-metre cut vinyl on a wall, which is most of what a small studio needs from a mark.

Icons

One icon per class the studio runs, plus a few for the app and the shop. All of them are the same weight of line inside the same circle, so a new class only needs one more drawing rather than a new system.

The icons in use as story highlight covers on the studio's Instagram.

Green on black, and nothing else. A small studio cannot afford a palette it has to explain.

Things members hold

Tote bags, towels, bottles and stickers — the parts of the identity that leave the building. These get carried to work and photographed by members, which for a studio this size does more than any campaign would.
