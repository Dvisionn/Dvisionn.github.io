Darb

Identity for a cultural café inside a traditional Palestinian house from 1938.

Client
Darb — Café & Cultural Space
Year
2025
Scope
Logotype, Arabic lettering, packaging, signage
Meaning
Darb — path, or road

The house was built in 1938. Ten years later the family who owned it were exiled to Lebanon.

After they left, the town used the house as a school. Later it became a base for a Palestinian political party. Then it sat. In 2025 the grandson of the original owners reclaimed it legally and opened it as a café and cultural space.

He called it Darb — path, or road.

Drawing the word

The logotype is drawn, not set. I started with a broad nib and worked through the letterforms on paper before taking anything near a screen.
