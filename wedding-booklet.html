Wedding Songbook

A booklet of traditional Palestinian wedding songs, printed for the guests to sing from.

Client
Private commission
Year
2024
Scope
Editorial design, Arabic typesetting, print
Format
Saddle-stitched booklet

The client wanted his guests to sing together. So we printed them a book.

Inside are traditional Palestinian wedding songs — the ones for the henna night, the zaffeh, the bride leaving her family's house. Nearly everyone in the room knows a line or two of each and loses the rest halfway through. Put them on a page and a whole room can carry a whole song.

Where the pattern comes from

The borders and the cover motif are drawn from Palestinian tatreez — the cross-stitch embroidery worked on thobs, cushion covers and framed pieces. These are the references I had in front of me before drawing anything.

Setting it

One song per page, no exceptions, so nobody has to turn a page mid-verse. The type is set large and openly leaded because the book gets read in low light, held in one hand, by people who are also holding something else.

The cover carries a single tatreez motif in the same red as the page borders. Cross-stitch is already a grid, so it sits against type without any argument — and it is the pattern most of these families have on something at home.

Printed to be used at one event, then kept.
